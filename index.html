Featured Research Programmes

Building clinical AI that works beyond the laboratory.

My lab's research focuses on developing multimodal, trustworthy and clinically deployable AI, with a flagship focus on maternal and fetal health. We combine medical imaging, clinical data and physiological signals to build systems that are interpretable, generalisable and useful in real clinical practice.

Programme 01 Flagship

AI for Maternal & Fetal Health
